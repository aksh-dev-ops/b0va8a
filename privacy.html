Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information b0va8a Hotels collects when you visit our website or stay with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as when you make a reservation, send an enquiry or stay at the hotel:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and postal address.
  • Booking details: arrival and departure dates, room preferences, number of guests and special requests.
  • Payment information: card details, which are handled by our secure payment provider.
  • Communications: messages you send us through our contact form, by email or by phone.

When you browse our website, we may also collect technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the pages you visit.

How we use it

  • To process and manage your reservations and payments.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide guest services.
  • To personalise your stay based on preferences you share with us.
  • To send you offers and updates, but only where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To maintain, secure and improve our website and services.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to provide our services, with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as payment processors, booking systems and IT providers.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our guests and staff.
  • A successor business, if b0va8a Hotels is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

All third parties we work with are required to keep your information confidential and secure.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site and improve your experience. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect how parts of the site work.

Data retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, tax and reporting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work hard to safeguard your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information.
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them online. Reservations must be made by an adult.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
