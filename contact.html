FAQ

Frequently asked

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to availability.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes — private chauffeur transfers can be arranged. Let us know your flight details at least 24 hours before arrival.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is complimentary when you book directly through our website. It's served daily from 7:00 to 10:30 AM.

Are pets allowed?

We welcome small pets in selected rooms. Please contact reservations before booking so we can prepare for your companion.

What is your cancellation policy?

Most rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Special and non-refundable rates may differ — details are shown at booking.
